# HANNOVER MESSE 2026

> The world's leading industrial trade fair — where manufacturing, energy, and digital technologies meet. A practical guide to the 2026 edition for engineers and students.

---

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏢 **Exhibitors** | 4,000+ international companies |
| 👥 **Expected visitors** | ~130,000 from 150+ countries |
| 🎤 **Speakers** | 1,600 |
| 🌎 **Partner country 2026** | 🇧🇷 Brazil |
| 🧭 **New format** | 3 major thematic hubs + central networking spaces |
| 📍 **Location** | Messegelände Hannover |
| 🗓️ **Dates** | April 20 – 24, 2026 |

> **Lead theme:** *"Think Tech Forward"*
> **Opening ceremony:** April 19, 18:00 — Hannover Congress Centrum (HCC)

---

## Hall Organization

- **Hydrogen Technologies** — Hall 11
- **Industrial Supply & Engineering Solutions** — Hall 17
- **Industrial AI** — Hall 27
- **Energy Infrastructure & Storage Technologies** — Hall 12

---

## 2026 Thematic Hubs

### 1. 🤖 Automation & Digitalization
- Smart manufacturing, robotics, industrial AI
- Digital platforms, cybersecurity
- Digital twins, IoT

### 2. ⚡ Energy & Industrial Infrastructure
- Hydrogen, electrification
- Grid technologies, sustainable power systems

### 3. 🔬 Research & Technology Transfer
- Breakthrough innovations
- Science-industry collaboration
- Startups and scale-ups

## Networking Formats

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| **Masterclasses** | High-level technical sessions |
| **Round Tables** | Peer discussion within the industry |
| **Speed Datings** | Quick matchmaking with companies / recruiters |
| **Center Stage** | Talks by leaders from industry, politics, and science |

> 💡 Each hub has a **dedicated central meeting space**.
