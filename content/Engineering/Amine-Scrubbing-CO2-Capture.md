# 🧪 Amine Scrubbing for CO₂ Capture (MEA)

> If PSA is the pressure play and TSA the temperature play, amine scrubbing is the **chemistry play** — the workhorse of post-combustion CO₂ capture and the backbone of blue hydrogen (SMR+CCS).

---

## What It Does

Amine absorption removes **CO₂** (and H₂S, if present) from a gas stream via gas–liquid contact at moderate temperature. The amine solvent absorbs the CO₂ chemically; then the solvent is regenerated by heating, releasing a **concentrated CO₂ stream** ready for compression and storage.

> ⚠️ It does **not** deliver ultra-pure H₂ on its own. After amine scrubbing, the gas still contains H₂, CO, and some CH₄ — if your spec is 99.9% H₂, you still need a polishing step (typically [PSA](/Engineering/PSA)).

---

## The Two Columns

### 1. Absorption Tower (MEA)

The gas stream flows upward through the absorber, counter-current to the descending amine solvent. CO₂ reacts with the amine and is absorbed into the liquid phase. The cleaned gas exits the top.

- **Capture points:** post-shift, reformer flue gas, or even PSA tail gas
- **Product:** a CO₂-rich solvent and a CO₂-lean gas

### 2. Regenerator Column

The CO₂-rich solvent is heated with steam to reverse the reaction. The CO₂ is released as a **capture stream** (relatively pure), and the regenerated amine is recycled back to the absorber.

- This is the energy heart of the process — the reboiler consumes most of the operating cost.

---

## Energy Demand — The Numbers That Matter

| Solvent | Reboiler duty |
|---|---|
| 30% MEA (standard) | **3.2–4.2 GJ/tCO₂** |
| Advanced solvents | **2.2–2.5 GJ/tCO₂** |

The thermal integration story is the interesting part: in an SMR plant, part of this heat can come from **hot process streams and furnace steam**. In one case study:

- **~50% of CO₂ captured** using only available heat, no external utilities
- **70–80% captured** with better solvents/operating conditions

This is what makes blue hydrogen credible — the capture energy is partially "free" because the plant already produces heat.

---

## Why Amine Scrubbing Wins

- ✅ Captures CO₂ at **strategic points** in the plant (post-shift, flue gas, tail gas)
- ✅ Delivers a **concentrated CO₂ stream** — the right feed for compression, transport, and storage
- ✅ Proven at scale — decades of industrial operation
- ✅ The **standard route for SMR+CCS** (blue H₂)

---

## Limitations

| Limitation | Implication |
|---|---|
| **High CAPEX** | Large columns, reboilers, solvent inventory |
| **High thermal OPEX** | Regeneration steam dominates operating cost |
| **Solvent management** | Corrosion, carryover, degradation, reclaiming |
| **Not a final polish** | Fuel-grade H₂ (>99.9%) still needs PSA or membranes |

---

## How It Fits the CO₂ Story

Amine scrubbing sits between the separation family and thermal integration:

- **Separation family:** [PSA](/Engineering/PSA) (pressure), [TSA](/Engineering/Temperature-Swing-Adsorption) (temperature), MEA (chemistry)
- **Thermal lever:** the reboiler duty (3.2–4.2 GJ/tCO₂) is exactly what [Pinch Analysis & Heat Exchanger Networks](/Engineering/Pinch-Analysis-HENs) optimize — recovering heat from flue gases and shift reactors to preheat feed gas and amine solvent

For the full hydrogen picture, see [SMR](/Engineering/SMR) and the [CO₂ value chain](/Engineering/CO2-Value-Chain).
