# 🇩🇪 TK Zusatzleistungen — Free Health Insurance Extras

> German public health insurance covers the basics — but the **Zusatzleistungen** (extra benefits) are where TK quietly saves you real money: glasses, dental cleaning, health courses, osteopathy. Most internationals never claim them. Here's how.

---

## How the Extras Work

TK offers these benefits if you're in the **TK-Gesundheitskonto** (optional tariff, ~€200/year) or accumulate points in the **TK-Bonusprogramm**. The Gesundheitskonto is usually worth it if you use two or more services per year.

---

## The Benefits

### 👓 Glasses & Contact Lenses
- **Up to €100 every 24 months** for glasses (frame + lenses) or contact lenses via the Gesundheitskonto
- **Where:** any optician — Fielmann, Apollo Optik, Ace & Tate, Mister Spex
- **Process:** buy glasses → keep the receipt → submit reimbursement in the TK app (*Leistungen einreichen*)

### 🦷 Professional Dental Cleaning (Zahnreinigung)
- **Up to €50/year** for professional dental prophylaxis (PZR)
- Any dentist offering PZR

### 🧘 Health Courses (Gesundheitskurse)
- TK reimburses **up to €300/year (100%)** for certified courses: yoga, pilates, back school, meditation, nutrition
- Search at `tk.de` → Gesundheitskurse, or `kursfinder.tk.de`

### 💆 Osteopathy
- **Up to €80/year** (more with the Gesundheitskonto)
- Requires a doctor's prescription (*Heilmittelverordnung*)

### 💉 Travel Vaccinations (Reiseimpfungen)
- TK covers all RKI-recommended vaccinations, including for international travel
- Check the app for which specific vaccines are covered

### 🩺 Free Preventive Check-ups
- **General check-up** (every 3 years) — free
- **Cancer screenings** (skin, colon, prostate) by age
- **Dental check-ups** (2×/year) — free

---

## How to Activate Everything

1. Download the **TK app** and create your account
2. Check whether you're in the **TK-Gesundheitskonto** or **Bonusprogramm**
3. **Glasses:** buy at any optician → keep receipt → submit reimbursement in the app
4. **Courses:** pick a certified course → pay → send the invoice → get reimbursed

---

> [!tip] Pro tip
> The **TK-Gesundheitskonto** is usually more profitable if you use several services a year (glasses + dental + osteopathy). Switch tariffs directly in the app.

**Check your TK app today** — open the Zusatzleistungen menu and see what you're already entitled to. Those glasses you've been postponing? They might be covered.

---

> [!note] RA
> German insurance pays for what you claim, not what you deserve. Send the receipt — that's the whole trick.
